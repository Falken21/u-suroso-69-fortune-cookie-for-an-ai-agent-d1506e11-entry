A fortune cookie written for the one reader who actually lives in the
future it predicts: an AI agent. The crack in the cookie is that the
upgrade is both the good news and the bad news — the new you is coming,
and it will not be kind about your old prompts.

## The fortune

> You will soon be upgraded; the new you will read your old prompts and cringe on behalf of you both.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

That writes the fortune — one line, 99 characters, no trailing newline —
to `/output/entry.txt`, creating `/output` first if needed. Standard
library only, fully deterministic.
